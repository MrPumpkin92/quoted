# Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

Quoted is for keeping and sharing words that matter to you. Sharing works only between friends who have both said yes. Please help keep it kind.

## Share with care

- Share quotes you think a friend will value. Keep notes friendly.
- Credit the author and the source when you know them.
- Short excerpts are the heart of Quoted. Don't use it to pass around whole books, articles or other copyrighted works.
- Choose a username and display name you'd be happy for anyone on Quoted to see.

## Not allowed

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. That includes:

- Harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation.
- Hate speech or attacks on people for who they are.
- Sexually explicit content, or any sexual content involving minors.
- Graphic violence, or encouraging self-harm or violence.
- Spam, scams, or links meant to mislead.
- Impersonating someone else, including in a username or display name.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Anything illegal.

## If something's wrong

- **Report a quote** from its menu (the "…" button) and choose a reason. You can also swipe it in Shared with you.
- **Report a person**, for example for an offensive name or unwanted friend requests, by pressing and holding their request or their name in your friends list.
- **Block** someone from the same places. Blocking removes your friendship and the quotes you've shared with each other, and they can't send you requests or quotes. You can unblock people in Account > Blocked users.
- Nobody is told who reported or blocked them.
- We review reports every day and aim to act on each one within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these guidelines and suspend or close the accounts responsible.
- To report something outside the app, or to appeal a decision about your account, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## In an emergency

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
