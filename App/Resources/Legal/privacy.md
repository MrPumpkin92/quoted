# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

Quoted is built to keep the words that move you, not to collect data about you. This policy explains what we store, why, who can see it and how to delete it. "We" means the developer of Quoted, the seller shown on its App Store page.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| An account ID that Quoted creates when you tap Get started | To keep your library, friends and shares together. The account has no email address or password, and it isn't linked to your phone number or Apple ID. |
| Your username and display name (optional) | So friends can find you and see who sent a quote. |
| Your quotes (text, formatting, author, source), which ones are in your reminders, in order, and which ones you marked as favorites | To keep your library in sync, to show your reminders and widgets, and to show your favorites on your profile. |
| Your friends, friend requests and blocks | To let you share quotes with the people you choose and keep out the people you don't. |
| Quotes you share, with any note | To deliver them to the friends you picked. We also record when your friend first opens and saves each one, so their inbox can show what's new. |
| Reports you file | To review content and accounts that may break the Community Guidelines. A report keeps the reason, any details sent with it (such as the person's display name at the time), a copy of the reported quote (if any) and the reported username. It stays linked to your account until you delete your account. |
| A count of taps on "Search the web" | To decide whether to build quote discovery into the app. Each tap is stored as the event name and the hour it happened, with no account ID. What you type is never sent to us: the search opens Google in an in-app browser, and Google's privacy policy applies there. |

Reminder settings (how often, which hours and days) and your last five searches stay on your device.

## What we don't do

- No advertising and no ad tracking.
- No selling or renting your data.
- No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We don't read your quotes, except a copy attached to a report or when needed to keep the service running.

## Where your data lives

Quoted stores your account and content with Supabase, which hosts the database and sign-in service for us as our data processor. Data is encrypted in transit. Like any web service, our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs of requests (such as IP address, time and which account made the request) for security and troubleshooting.

On your iPhone, Quoted keeps a copy of your library so the app, reminders and widgets work offline, and keeps your sign-in in the iPhone's Keychain, on this device only. That sign-in is the only key to your account: if you delete the app or move to a new iPhone, the account can't be opened again. Quote images you choose to save go to your photo library; Quoted can add to it but can't see it.

## Who can see what

- Your quotes are private to you. The one exception is your choice: if you turn on "Show favorites to friends" on your profile, your friends can see the quotes you marked as favorites (text, formatting, author and source) and when you marked each one, and can save a copy of one to their own library; that copy is theirs. It's off until you turn it on and it covers only favorites. If you turn it off, friends stop seeing your favorites the next time their app loads your profile, and copies they already saved stay with them. People who aren't your friends, or who you blocked or who blocked you, never see them.
- Your username and display name are visible to people you're connected with (friends, friend requests, shared quotes and blocks) and to anyone who types your exact username. People you blocked, or who blocked you, can't find you by username.
- A quote you share is visible to you and the friend you sent it to, along with when they opened or saved it. They can save a copy to their own library, and that copy is theirs.
- People you block can't send you friend requests or quotes. They aren't told that you blocked them or reported them.

## Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete your account in Account > Delete account and we permanently delete your sign-in, profile, quotes, friendships, requests, blocks and every quote shared with you or by you from our servers. Copies that friends already saved to their own libraries stay with them. Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's backups until those backups expire.

We also delete accounts that are no longer used, with all of the data above: an account that never chose a username is deleted 7 days after it was created, and an account that hasn't been opened for 12 months is deleted. Deletion is final. Friends of a deleted account simply no longer see it.

Reports are kept for safety after an account is deleted: a report you filed is kept without your account, and a report about you keeps the reported username and content. "Search the web" counts carry no account ID, so there is nothing to delete.

Deleting your account removes your cached library, widget data, recent searches and scheduled reminders from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them too, and a reinstalled Quoted starts over with a new account: the previous one can no longer be opened, and is deleted after 12 months without use. To remove your data right away, delete your account in Account > Delete account before you delete the app.

## Children

Quoted is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app first.
