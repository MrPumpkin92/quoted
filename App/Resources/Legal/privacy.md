# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

Quoted is built to keep the words that move you, not to collect data about you. This policy explains what we store, why, who can see it and how to delete it. "We" means the developer of Quoted, the seller shown on its App Store page.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address and password | To sign you in and to send account emails (confirmation and password reset). Your password is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in service. |
| Your username and display name (optional) | So friends can find you and see who sent a quote. |
| Your quotes (text, formatting, author, source) and which ones are in your reminders, in order | To keep your library in sync and to show your reminders and widgets. |
| Your friends, friend requests and blocks | To let you share quotes with the people you choose and keep out the people you don't. |
| Quotes you share, with any note | To deliver them to the friends you picked. |
| Reports you file | To review content and accounts that may break the Community Guidelines. A report keeps the reason, a copy of the reported quote (if any) and the reported username. |
| A count of taps on "Search the web" | To decide whether to build quote discovery into the app. Each tap is stored as the event name and the hour it happened, with no account ID, so it can't be linked to you. What you searched for is never sent. |

Reminder settings (how often, which hours and days) stay on your device.

## What we don't do

- No advertising and no ad tracking.
- No selling or renting your data.
- No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We don't read your quotes, except a copy attached to a report or when needed to keep the service running.

## Where your data lives

Quoted stores your account and content with Supabase, which hosts the database and sign-in service for us as our data processor. Data is encrypted in transit. Like any web service, our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs of requests (such as IP address and time) for security and troubleshooting.

On your iPhone, Quoted keeps a copy of your library so the app, reminders and widgets work offline, and keeps your sign-in in the iPhone's Keychain, on this device only. Quote images you choose to save go to your photo library; Quoted can add to it but can't see it.

## Who can see what

- Your quotes are private to you.
- Your username and display name are visible to other signed-in Quoted users.
- A quote you share is visible to you and the friend you sent it to. They can save a copy to their own library, and that copy is theirs.
- People you block can't send you friend requests or quotes. They aren't told that you blocked them or reported them.

## Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete your account in Account > Delete account and we permanently delete your sign-in, profile, quotes, friendships, requests, blocks and every quote shared with you or by you from our servers. Copies that friends already saved to their own libraries stay with them. Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's backups until those backups expire.

Reports are kept for safety after an account is deleted: a report you filed is kept without your account, and a report about you keeps the reported username and content. "Search the web" counts are anonymous, so there is nothing to delete.

Signing out removes your cached library, widget data and scheduled reminders from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them too, and a reinstalled Quoted starts signed out.

## Children

Quoted is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app first.
