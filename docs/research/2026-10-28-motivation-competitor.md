# Motivation competitor research and positioning for the social redesign

Module 0 of the marketing-research sourcebook, researched 2026-10-28. Third-party revenue and install figures are estimates. Sophistication levels are our own rating on the sourcebook's 1 to 5 scale.

**Bottom line:** Motivation wins on scale and habit and loses on trust. Half of its negative reviews are about money, it shows no sources or creators, and it has no friends layer. Quoted should compete on visible provenance, a calm friends-only social layer and honest pricing. It should never compete on volume.

## 2. Competitor landscape map

No quote app combines verified sources, a friends layer and honest pricing. The leaders own habit and widgets. The social quote apps are tiny and unsourced.

| Competitor | Core Promise | Unique Mechanism | Target Avatar | Soph. (1-5) | Positioning Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Motivation, Monkey Taps | Daily positive quotes on widgets and reminders | 41-step onboarding quiz that personalizes a category feed | Self-improvers using quotes for mood and routine, often through hard times | 3 | No sources, no creator labels, no friends. Money practices burn users |
| I am, Monkey Taps | Affirmations on your Lock Screen | Same quiz, widget and reminder formula | Affirmation users | 3 | Share button only. Lifetime costs $149.99 |
| Quotes: Daily Motivation | Quotes with widgets and nature sounds | Ambient sounds plus reminders | Casual motivation seekers | 2 | No author names, no accounts. $29.99 a year |
| Goodreads Quotes | Quotes from the books you read | 150M-member community database with tags and likes | Readers | 2 | Disclaims verification. Never shows who added a quote |
| GoodQuotes | Your profile becomes your quote collection | Follow, private groups, For You feed, 5-friend invite cap | Social quote savers | 3 | 15 ratings. No DMs, no sourcing, no listed report or block tools |
| Deepstash | Short idea and quote cards | Bite-size cards from books and articles | Self-learners | 3 | Reviewers flag missing sources. Up to $12.99 a month |
| Gratitude Plus, With Gratitude | Gratitude with your circle | Private circles, a feed, a widget of friends' entries | Wellness journalers | 3 | No quotes. Gratitude Plus lists no report or block tools |
| Readwise | Resurface your reading highlights | Resurfacing plus shareable textshots | Heavy readers | 4 | No friends layer. Textshots carry no contributor mark |
| QOTD | Scan and file book quotes | OCR with author, source and page fields | Book annotators | 3 | Single user. Provenance stops at the share sheet |

**Motivation.** Its strategic gap is trust. It already has every core feature Quoted has: widgets, reminders, favorites, collections and your own quotes. It shows no source, no creator and no verification, and reviewers who spot one misattributed line stop trusting the rest. It shares only outward as images and text, while users ask to send folders to friends and one reviewer asked for friends outright.

**Goodreads Quotes.** The largest quote community says plainly that it does not verify quotes, and its pages never show who added a quote. User tags that flag misattributed quotes prove the community sees the problem and has no tool for it. Quoted can own the verification label Goodreads declines to give.

**GoodQuotes.** The only direct quote-social rival proves demand and nothing more. With 15 ratings, no DMs, no sourcing and no visible safety tools, it leaves the friends-plus-provenance space empty.

**Gratitude Plus and With Gratitude.** They prove small private circles can sustain a daily wellness habit, and With Gratitude puts friends' entries on a Home Screen widget. Neither carries quotes, so the circle model is free to borrow.

**Readwise.** It owns "resurface what matters to you," which overlaps Quoted's Rotation. Its gap is social: textshots leave the app as images with no friend layer and no contributor mark.

**Saturated claims:**
1. "Daily motivation on your Home Screen." Every leader opens with widgets.
2. "Thousands of quotes, personalized for you." Volume plus an onboarding quiz.
3. "Build the habit." Streaks and reminders, which now draw guilt complaints.

## 3. Competitive gap analysis

Trust is the gap, and money is its loudest symptom. Misattribution is a quiet complaint, so provenance works as the proof of trust, not the headline pain.

**Evidence behind the gaps,** from 1,929 App Store reviews across US, GB, CA and AU:

| Signal | Number |
|---|---|
| Money complaints in 1-3 star reviews | 49%, rising to 55% in 2025-26. 58% of all 1-star reviews |
| Trial trap: charged a year when expecting a month, or after cancelling | 20%, rising to 28% in 2025-26 |
| Billing as the reason in explicit churn statements | 53% of 94 |
| Notification, alarm or streak annoyance as the churn reason | 31% of 94 |
| Misattributed or author-less quotes | About 1%, plus a 4% cluster on author control |
| Share of 1-2 star reviews in GB, CA and AU | About 20% in 2021-24, 30% in 2025, 36% in 2026 |

**Overcrowded angles:**
- Quote widgets on the Home and Lock Screen.
- Quiz-driven, personalized category feeds.
- Quote volume across dozens of categories.
- Streaks and reminders as habit fuel.
- Themes, fonts and photo backgrounds as the premium unlock.

**Underserved desires:**
- Know the quote is real and who actually said it.
- Share a collection with a friend, not just an image to followers.
- See the price before spending 15 minutes on setup.
- Control the content: mute an author, stop a topic, set the frequency, feel no guilt.
- Own my quotes: synced to an account and safe through every update.

**Open positioning territories:**
1. **Trusted quotes.** Provenance becomes the product: who said it, who added it, and whether the wording was checked. No consumer quote app shows all three, and Wikiquote's sourced, attributed and misattributed tiers give a ready model. Motivation cannot copy this without auditing its whole catalog.
2. **Friends, not followers.** A small private circle that sends quotes and playlists to each other. Motivation users ask for it, and Spotify's relationship-only Messages shows how to do it safely. Some Motivation fans value it for not being social media, so the circle must stay calm.
3. **The honest quote app.** No ads, no trial trap, no paywalled search, and an account that keeps your quotes safe. Billing drives most of Motivation's churn and its negative share is climbing. Honesty is a feature this market has stopped expecting.

**Most underserved sophistication level: Level 4.** The market speaks at Levels 2 and 3: more quotes, picked for you, on your widget. Motivation's unhappy reviewers are Level 4 buyers. They tried the category, paid for it, got charged for a year they did not want and watched free features move behind a paywall. "More quotes" no longer moves them. Certainty does: a sourced quote, a clear price, data that survives updates. Nobody speaks to them, and Quoted's seal and account-backed library answer exactly what past tools missed.

## 4. Differentiation pressure test

| Dimension | Competitors (consensus) | Quoted (potential) |
|---|---|---|
| Quote source | Unsourced catalog, sometimes generic or AI-sounding | Starter pack wording checked against public-domain sources |
| Attribution | An author name, if any, never questioned | Said-by on every quote, with a filled verified seal |
| Who added it | Hidden or absent | Created-by label, hollow for person-made quotes |
| Social | Share an image outward | Friends Feed, Inbox threads, shared playlists |
| Sharing | Image with an app tag | Share card that keeps provenance and carries no forced tag |
| Pricing | Quiz, then a 3-day trial, then a yearly charge | No ads and no paywall today |
| Privacy | Five data types used to track you, plus ad SDKs | Private by default and server-enforced. Only supabase-swift as a dependency |
| Data safety | Favorites lost on update without an account | Account-backed sync for quotes, favorites and playlists |
| Notifications | Streak nagging and an alarm that outlives the app | Rotation-driven reminders at the user's pace |
| Readability | Widget and Watch truncation, text that jumps size | The whole quote fits on screen in the quote view |
| Safety tools | None listed by GoodQuotes, Gratitude Plus or Literal | Visible block, report and participant-only threads |

On the surface, Quoted sounds like Motivation. Quotes, favorites, widgets and reminders are table stakes, and a Level 4 buyer reading "keep the quotes that matter" sees one more quote app. Pitched that way, Quoted loses to 1.1M ratings and weekly releases every time.

Quoted is genuinely different in three places. It tells you whether a quote is real and who put it there. It lets friends exchange quotes in a private space. It never traps anyone's money or data. What stops a Level 4 buyer mid-scroll is one line that names their past disappointment: every quote shows who said it, who added it, and whether anyone checked. Lead with it and show the seal in the first screenshot.

## 7. Build implications for the redesign

| Tab or feature | What Motivation does or lacks | What Quoted must do to be uniquely better | What to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feed | No people at all. A vertical feed of generic quotes with ads | Friends-only, chronological posts and shares, each showing its seal. The empty state invites one friend | Algorithmic ranking, public reach, like counts as status, a push per post |
| Library | Categories and themes partly paywalled. Collections private. No sources | Keep today's Library. The verified public library stays free, playlists are shareable, every row shows said-by, created-by and seal | Paywalling categories, search or anything once free |
| Inbox | Nothing. Users ask to send folders to friends | Friends-only threads for text, quotes and playlists. Server-enforced participant-only access with tests. Message requests, block, report, a switch to turn messages off | Open DMs with strangers, contact-book upload, automated invite reminders |
| Profile | No profile. No account, so favorites vanish on update | Public playlists, favorites and favorite quotes, each with a hide toggle and private by default. Settings and in-app account deletion tucked below | Follower counts, default-public sections, a settings maze |
| Search | Premium only | Free search, filterable by author, seal status and creator. Keep the current button placement | Gating search behind a trial |
| Quote view | Larger text loses the theme, widget and Watch cut text, size jumps on save | Fit the whole quote on screen by scaling type to the space, with seal and labels visible without scrolling | Truncation, scroll-to-read, size jumps between views |
| Created-by label | Own quotes carry no made-by mark. Famous quotes carry no source | Filled seal for verified. Hollow label with the creator's name for person-made. The label survives shares, Inbox snapshots and share images | Wording that reads as "my friend said this". Dropping the label on share |
| Share card | Auto-tags the app on posts. A bug changes the background | Keep the look and the seal on the image, with no forced watermark or tag | Self-promotional tagging |
| Notifications | Streak nagging, unreliable reminders, an alarm that outlives the app | One notification budget shared by reminders and social. Social pushes stay quiet by default. Respect Focus | Streak guilt, system alarms, a push per reaction |
| Safety | Nothing to moderate today | Apple Guideline 1.2: filter, report, block, published contact, zero-tolerance terms, action on reports within 24 hours | Shipping social without block and report, which App Review rejects |

**Open decisions for the council:**
- Whether a friends-only feed with likes triggers Apple's 13+ Social Media age rating.
- Whether to cap friends. Locket caps at 20 and Duolingo caps Friend Streak slots at 5.
- Whether the Feed shows reactions at all, given fans who value Motivation for not being social media.

**Do not copy:**
1. A 41-step quiz before any value, ending in a paywall.
2. A "free" trial that converts to a yearly charge without a reminder.
3. Moving free features behind a paywall or dropping lifetime purchases.
4. Ads, upsell pop-ups and auto-tagging the app on users' posts.
5. Streak guilt, notifications you cannot turn off and alarms that outlive the app.
6. Dislike and topic filters that do nothing.
7. Tracking users with five data types, and a privacy policy that allows sharing data for model training.
8. Storing quotes without an account, so an update wipes them.
9. Unsourced, generic or gender-assuming quotes.
