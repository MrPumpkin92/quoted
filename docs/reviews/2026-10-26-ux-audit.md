# UX audit: Quoted (whole app, report only)

Date 2026-10-26.

- **Device:** iPhone Air simulator, iOS 26.5.
- **Build:** Debug-Local at main `8f3f955` (= TestFlight build 5), pointed at the isolated local stack (127.0.0.1:55321).
- **Mode:** `--report-only`. The founder asked for "other things in the app that need to be worked on" for a morning review, so nothing in this report was changed in code.
- **Open product questions:** went to an LLM Council, as the founder asked (`docs/decisions/2026-10-26-audit-product-decisions-council.md`).

## Product model

- **What:** a personal quote library that comes back to you. Reminders (local notifications several times a day) and widgets show quotes you saved; friends can share quotes into each other's inbox.
- **Who:** people who collect lines that move them, from books, talks and friends; the brand is accuracy (verified public-domain starter quotes).
- **Primary goal:** see the words that matter to you again, every day, without effort.
- **Core screens:** Library, Reminders, the notification and widget themselves, the full-screen quote. Secondary: Friends, shared inbox, Search, Add/Edit. Tertiary: Account, profiles, legal.
- **Most frequent actions:**
  1. reading a notification or widget;
  2. tapping into the full-screen quote;
  3. adding a quote;
  4. adjusting Reminders;
  5. sharing to a friend.

**Environment note.** Two save attempts on the local stack hit `57014 statement timeout`. The Docker VM has 2 CPUs and 4 GB, shared by three Supabase stacks and another project's SQL server running at 81% CPU. This is an environment artifact, not an app bug. The app handled it correctly: an error toast, then a retry that found the saved row.

## Needs a product decision

Each item went to the council. The full record is in `docs/decisions/2026-10-26-audit-product-decisions-council.md`. The council recommends:

| # | Decision | Recommendation | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account durability | Optional "Back up with Apple" (Sign in with Apple linked to the anonymous account) in Account, and "Sign in with Apple" on the first onboarding screen. Switch accounts only when the current one is empty; never merge. Revoke the token on deletion. No export/import, and don't keep the cache on restore. | Before App Store submission |
| 2 | Support address | support@ on a Quoted domain, forwarding to the Gmail. One constant feeds Account, the legal pages and the App Store URL. | Before submission |
| 3 | Reminder sound | A Notifications section with a Sound picker: Off / First of the day (default) / Every reminder. | Before submission |
| 4 | Friend alerts | A "Friend activity" toggle, separate from reminders, shipped with the tap-destination fix (F5). | Before submission |
| 5 | Sent record and unsend | "Shared between you" on a friend's profile, in both directions. Unsend: never. | Build 6 / never |
| 6 | Usernames | Say "You can't change this later." No rename in v1. | Before submission |
| 7 | Shuffle | Deal like a deck, reshuffle each pass, no repeat across the boundary. | Before submission |
| 8 | Search chips | Now: show only chips with matches. Build 6/7: the chips become the catalog themes. | Before submission / build 7 |
| 9 | Nudge | The last queued reminder carries "Open Quoted to keep reminders coming". | Build 6 |
| 10 | Profiles | The sender's name opens their profile everywhere. Favorites stay private by default; ask once when the first friend request is accepted. Move the switch up a level in Account. | Build 6 |

## Not changed on purpose

- Email sign-in screens and Sign out stay dormant behind `AccountMode` (the 2026-10-20 decision).
- The iOS 26 tab bar collapsing to one button on the Search tab is system behaviour.
- The planner's 60-slot cap and the nudge mechanism are the 2026-10-21 decision. The design for new quotes keeps them.
