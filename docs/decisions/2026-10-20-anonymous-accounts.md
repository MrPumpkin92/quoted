# Decision: no sign-in, one anonymous account per iPhone (2026-10-20)

> Superseded in part by `2026-10-21-v1-launch-decisions.md`: the account is now created by Welcome's Get started rather than at launch, a lost or deleted account returns to Welcome, and abandoned anonymous accounts are cleaned up. The notes below on automatic account creation and the "started a new account" toast describe the 2026-10-20 build.

## Decision (founder, 2026-10-20)

The founder asked to "remove the entire log in system for now". Email confirmation was blocking testing: Supabase's built-in email reaches only the Supabase team, and custom SMTP is not set up yet.

Quoted now shows no sign-in screens. With no stored session, the app calls Supabase anonymous sign-in (`AuthService.signInAnonymously()`) and goes straight to "Choose a username", then the starter pack and the main tabs. The account is a real Supabase Auth user with `is_anonymous = true` and no email or password.

This keeps everything the server owns: data in Postgres, RLS, friends, sharing, reports and account deletion. RLS is unchanged. Anonymous users have the role `authenticated`, and the policies treat them like any other user (`supabase/tests/database/10_anonymous_user.test.sql`).

The switch is `AccountMode.current` in `App/Stores/SessionStore.swift`, set to `.anonymous`. The email screens (`AuthFlowView`, `NewPasswordView`) and the email methods stay in the repo, unreachable while the mode is anonymous.

## Alternative considered and rejected

- **A local-only app without accounts.** It means rewriting the stores and repositories around on-device storage, and it loses friends, sharing and reports, which need an identity on the server.
- **One shared demo account** that every install signs in to. Everyone would see and change everyone's quotes, friends and shares, and one deletion would delete it for all.
- **Keep email sign-in.** Email confirmation is the blocker this decision removes.

## Consequences

- **No sign-in UI:** while the account is created, RootView shows the launch screen. If that fails (offline on the first launch, or anonymous sign-ins turned off on the server: "Quoted can't create your account right now: anonymous sign-ins are turned off on the server."), the launch screen shows the error with Try again.
- **Sign out is hidden** in Account and on Choose a username. It is offered only when `AccountMode.current == .emailPassword` and the account is not anonymous (`SessionStore.canSignOut`). In this build no account gets Sign out, including a leftover or DEBUG email session. Nothing could sign back in to an anonymous account, so signing out would lose it for good. To leave the DEBUG demo account locally, launch with `-UITestReset` or reinstall the app.
- **Delete account is kept.** App Store 5.1.1(v) requires it, and Apple's account-deletion FAQ covers accounts an app creates automatically ("guest" accounts). After a deletion the app starts a new, empty account and shows Choose a username.
- **The account belongs to this iPhone:** the session lives in this iPhone's Keychain (`ThisDeviceOnly`), and the install marker clears it on the first launch after an install. A reinstall or a new iPhone therefore starts a new account. The old account's rows stay on the server, unreachable. There is no cleanup job yet (Supabase has no automatic cleanup of anonymous users). A future one must go by last activity, not account age, or it deletes people who still use the app.
- **Lost sessions:** `AppPreferences.hadAnonymousSession` records that this install's last session was an anonymous account the user did not leave on purpose (Sign out and a successful Delete account clear it). It lives in `UserDefaults.standard`, which iOS deletes with the app. When the SDK drops a session (the server no longer accepts its refresh token), or the server rejects one the SDK has already dropped, the app starts a new anonymous account and, once that account exists and the app is in front (a replacement made during a background refresh waits for the next activation), shows the toast "Your session ended, so Quoted started a new account on this iPhone." A reinstall never shows it. If no new account can start, the launch screen shows the error with Try again, never a bare spinner.
  - A `.notSignedIn` answer while the SDK still holds the anonymous session keeps the account. Before the session is ready (no cached profile, or the empty-library check without the onboarding flag), the launch screen shows the error with Try again. Otherwise the app stays where it is: background refreshes fail quietly, and actions show the toast "Your session has ended. Try again in a moment." until the SDK refreshes or drops the session.
  - If the SDK drops an anonymous session during Delete account, the account could not be deleted: it stays on the server, unreachable. The app starts a new account and shows the same "Your session ended, so Quoted started a new account on this iPhone." toast.
- **Moderation is weaker:** deleting an abusive anonymous user under Authentication > Users doesn't keep them out. A reinstall creates a new account with a new username, and no earlier block covers it (blocks go with the deleted account). Supabase recommends a CAPTCHA (hCaptcha or Turnstile) for anonymous sign-ins; there is none yet.
- **Rate limit:** hosted Auth allows 30 anonymous sign-ins an hour per IP by default (Authentication > Rate Limits). Testers who share one network share that limit. The local stack allows 1000 (`anonymous_users` in `supabase/config.toml`), because every UI test run creates an account.
- **The first launch needs a network** to create the account.
- **Hosted setup:** turn on Authentication > Sign In / Providers > "Allow anonymous sign-ins" (a founder dashboard step, `docs/system-design.md` §10 and the "Anonymous sign-ins" step of the `scripts/deploy-backend.sh` checklist). Until then every new install stops on the launch screen with the "turned off" message. Once it is on, the hosted Security Advisor lists the INFO entry "Anonymous sign-ins enabled" (`0012_auth_allow_anonymous_sign_ins`). That entry is expected and accepted: the policies deliberately don't check `is_anonymous`, and `supabase/tests/database/10_anonymous_user.test.sql` proves the parity, so don't add `is_anonymous` checks or turn anonymous sign-ins off to clear it. Locally, `enable_anonymous_sign_ins = true` in `supabase/config.toml` takes effect after `supabase stop && supabase start`.
- **Email steps are not needed while accounts are anonymous:** custom SMTP, the email rate limit, the email templates, URL Configuration and "Confirm email". They come back with email sign-in.
- **App Review:** there is no login, so untick "Sign-in required" in App Review Information. The reviewer gets a fresh account on first launch. Report and Block need another person, so the founder keeps a helper account whose username goes in the review notes; the reviewer adds it from Friends (`docs/app-store-submission.md` §8).
- **Privacy and Terms still describe email accounts. The founder decides.** `App/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` declares Email Address and the App Privacy label has an Email Address row (`docs/app-store-submission.md` §6), but no email is collected while accounts are anonymous. Lines that no longer match, with suggested wording:
  - `terms.md:5` "By creating an account or using Quoted, you agree to them…" → "By using Quoted, you agree to them and to the Community Guidelines."
  - `terms.md:9` "You need an email address and a password to use Quoted. Keep your password private." → "Quoted creates an account for you on this iPhone the first time you open it. It has no email or password, so it can't be recovered if you delete the app or move to a new iPhone."
  - `privacy.md:11` the "Your email address and password" row → "An account ID for this iPhone | To keep your library, friends and shares tied to you. Quoted doesn't ask for an email address or a password."
  - `privacy.md:32` "keeps your sign-in in the iPhone's Keychain, on this device only." → "keeps your account's sign-in key in the iPhone's Keychain, on this device only. It is the only way into your account: if you delete the app or move to a new iPhone, Quoted starts a new account."
  - `privacy.md:43` "we permanently delete your sign-in, profile, quotes…" → "we permanently delete your account, profile, quotes…". Consider adding: "Delete your account in Account > Delete account before you delete the app. Otherwise the account stays on our servers but can't be opened again, and we can't confirm it is yours, so we can't delete it on request." An anonymous account has no email, so nothing shows who owns it: support must not delete one because someone emails its username.
  - `privacy.md:47` "Signing out removes… and a reinstalled Quoted starts signed out." → "Deleting your account removes your cached library, widget data, recent searches and scheduled reminders from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them too, and a reinstalled Quoted starts a new, empty account."
- **DEBUG-only password sign-in:** UI tests and `scripts/sign-in-local-demo.sh` still need a known account. DEBUG builds whose Supabase URL host is `127.0.0.1` or `localhost` read `QUOTED_DEBUG_SIGNIN_EMAIL` and `QUOTED_DEBUG_SIGNIN_PASSWORD` from the launch environment. With no stored session (for example after `-UITestReset`) they sign in with that email and password instead of creating an anonymous account. A failure shows on the launch screen with Try again, with no anonymous fallback. With a stored session the variables are ignored. Release builds and hosted URLs never read them.
- **Reversal:** set `AccountMode.current = .emailPassword` in `App/Stores/SessionStore.swift` (Sign out then comes back for email accounts), then redo the email dashboard steps (custom SMTP, the email rate limit, templates, URL Configuration, "Confirm email") and restore the email wording in the legal pages and the privacy label. To let people keep their data, prefer linking each anonymous user to an email with `auth.update(user: UserAttributes(email:))` (then a password) over making them sign up again.
